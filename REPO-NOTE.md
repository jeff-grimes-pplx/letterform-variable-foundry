# What is in this folder (and what is not)

The project repo is at its 5 GiB cap (a 1.5 MB text commit was rejected), so this folder carries only `HANDOFF.md` (an abridged copy: the embedded film receipts are cut to their ffprobe/SHA-256 block, caption table and verify summary; the full v4 handoff is asset c60a91fc-fddf-43e4-83f2-442152b8c40b), `STATUS.md` and this note. The complete text tree — brief, license, provenance, acceptance, QA receipts, capture/assembly/check scripts, specimen and app source, font source, the v4 film logs — is the thread asset `A4-letterform-source-1.103-v4.zip` (asset ZIP_V4_ASSET, ZIP_V4_FILES files, ZIP_V4_BYTES B, sha256 ZIP_V4_SHA; v4 take 4 of 21 Oct 2026, 60 s films under standard v4; it supersedes `A4-letterform-source-1.103-v3.zip`, asset 5b2e48ae-2211-4f0c-b47a-8a9bd43cfc65, 220 files, `A4-letterform-source-1.103.zip`, asset c13ac92e-47df-4a53-833b-8b3a39f1604d, 150 files, and `A4-letterform-source-1.102.zip`, 75 files) of session ed009d18-115d-42d0-aad5-d77280541179, where the binaries are also delivered with SHA-256s listed in `HANDOFF.md`:

- `fonts/Apertide[wdth,wght].ttf`, `.woff2`, `fonts/static/*.ttf`, `fonts/Apertide-fonts-1.103.zip` — `python3 source/build.py all` rebuilds them from `source/`.
- `specimen/Apertide-Specimen.pdf` and the generated `specimen/index.html` — `python3 specimen/make_specimen.py` then `node specimen/print.mjs`.
- `app/fonts/` (the woff2 and the static zip the app serves) and the generated `app/data/apertide-data.json` (Drawing-view skeletons, `python3 source/export_app_data.py`) — the app is deployed privately as asset 0c5239d4-0beb-41bd-8712-5e64691330ba (bundle hash 8408de75…, v4d).
- `films/letterform-60s-captioned.mp4` (release candidate, v4 take 5, asset f8d03a09-71b2-4408-ab42-81669d994a9f) and `films/letterform-60s.mp4` (clean, asset 7b8e7bd8-f971-4d1c-9d3f-7caf47a6e61a), their master, the v3 pair (`films/letterform-45s-captioned.mp4` asset 1b0a3285-abb6-4829-9832-1f919aae101c, `films/letterform-45s.mp4` asset 2ce1f3d1-8526-4436-bf0c-3a3f127736e0; v2.1 pair under `films/v21/`), the superseded `Letterform-60s/30s/45s.mp4`, and the frame directories (`a4-scratch/work60v4/` for the v4 films) — `films/README.md` gives the regeneration order; the v4 capture, camera, caption, verify and review logs are `films/logs60v4/` in the zip (the per-beat stills are not).
- `qa/app-shots/`, `qa/fontbakery-static.json` (3 MB) and `qa/pair-exhaustive.json` — the `.txt`/`.html` versions of those receipts are here.
