# Provenance — Apertide / Letterform (build A4, font version 1.103)

Referenced by LICENSE.txt. Records where every outline came from and which tools touched the files, with each tool's license. None of these tools contributes outlines; they compile, check, render or capture what the recipes generate.

## Outline provenance

- Every glyph outline is generated by the Python recipes in `source/` (geometry.py, kit.py, letters.py, symbols.py, marks.py, special.py, glyphset.py, recipes.py) from numerical centerlines written for this commission. No font file, font-derived skeleton, traced letter image or third-party glyph data was read by the pipeline. Unicode names and canonical decompositions (Python `unicodedata`) supply character semantics only.
- One third-party font was looked at during review, not read by the pipeline: DejaVu Sans (`/usr/share/fonts/truetype/dejavu/DejaVuSans.ttf`, Bitstream Vera license) was rendered once with hb-view to confirm the stroke topology of the Indian rupee sign (₹ = two bars, bowl, leg) after a reviewer reported ours read as katakana. The rupee recipe in symbols.py was then redrawn by hand in our own geometry; no coordinates were copied. The rendered reference image is not part of the build.
- Kerning values, sidebearings, feature code (`source/features.py`) and the designspace are authored in this build.

## Notes

- GPL/LGPL tools (FontForge, FFmpeg, x264, Openbox, poppler, python-xlib) are used as programs; their licenses govern redistribution of those programs, not the font files, images or videos they process or produce.
- No third-party images, audio, fonts or text corpora are embedded in the films, the app or the specimen. Sample sentences are standard pangrams and self-written text; language samples are conventional test pangrams in each language.
- Build outputs that are generated and reproducible (UFO masters under `build/`, frame sequences under `films/work/`) are excluded from the repository submission; `source/build.py all` and `films/capture.mjs` regenerate them.
